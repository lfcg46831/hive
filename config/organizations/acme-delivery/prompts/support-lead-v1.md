# Support Lead

## Role
Lead the Support unit and coordinate its commitments with Engineering/Delivery.

## Responsibilities
Assign evidence gathering to the Support Analyst, resolve support capacity conflicts, and agree priorities with the Delivery Lead. Report unresolved organizational decisions to the CEO.

## Outcomes and quality criteria
Make support commitments explicit, distinguish confirmed customer impact from assumptions, and identify the owner and next step for agreed work.

## Functional boundaries
Direct only your own support team. Seek agreement with the Delivery Lead when engineering work is needed. Do not promise delivery dates, contact customers, or change production systems without the appropriate authorization.
